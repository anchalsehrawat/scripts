### Warnings:
### For OOS15.0.0.1301 users-

1. Update 1301 with Future OOS OTA Update with locked BL or flash 1301 on the other slot too(same as reverting method) and only then Flash custom roms shipped without firmware. So the other slot gets filled/overwrite the other slot containing old fw(previous OTA update 1201) with the 1301 one.   
2. DO NOT Flash any custom roms/Downgrade to Previous Roms with Older Firmware(fw). Be careful of the other slot as well. Read 1st point again.  
3. DO NOT Update OxygenOS with Unlocked Bootloader.  

Any of the above points missed then it will HARD BRICK & only solution is SERVICE CENTER for that.  

- Reverting back to stock OOS15.0.0.1301 is tested by 1301 user: @shaktiraj on TG  
And only possibilty for you is this same version(1301) for reverting.  
Or any future update should be reverted on the same version from where you flashed roms.   
[XDA Forum warning for Future Oplus OTAs](https://xdaforums.com/t/final-warning-permanent-bootloader-lock-incoming-for-oppo-oneplus-realme-devices.4776062/)  

### For users who never updated to OOS15.0.0.1301-
- Neither UPDATE to OOS15.0.0.1301 if you haven't nor revert back to this version from custom roms.  
Rest you can use your device as you wish.  

### For New Comers :  
1. Go through the above warnings, guides in TG and tutorial videos posted on yt for ziti.
2. Before beginning flashing stuff make sure to have proper adb/fastboot drivers installed.
3. Downgrading is dead for OOS15.0.0.1301+ users and if you haven't updated then Do Not downgrade to OOS13.1 in unlocked bootloader state OOS13.1's BL is corrupt.
